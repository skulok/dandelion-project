The Dandelion Project

What is the Dandelion Project?

The Dandelion Project strives to share nature-based mindfulness with communities through guided experiences.


What is mindfulness?

Mindfulness is the practice of being present and fully engaged with whatever we are doing, without distraction or judgment.


What happens when nature and mindfulness are combined?

When nature and mindfulness are combined, it can create a powerful experience that promotes

  • relaxation
  • reduces stress
  • enhances overall well-being

Being in nature can help us feel more connected to the world around us, while mindfulness can help us appreciate and fully experience the beauty of nature. Next time you are outdoors, consider taking a moment to practice mindfulness and connect with the natural world around you in one of the following ways
  1. Sit quietly and listen to the sounds around you
  2. Observe the colors and textures of plants and trees
  3. Take slow, deep breaths and focus on the sensation of air entering and leaving your body
